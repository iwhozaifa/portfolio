problem: CS332 Design and Analysis of Algorithms students need notes that are clear, correct and usable the week before a midterm.
built:
  - "A study site with nine topics, from logs and sums to recurrences. Each topic has four study modes, as tabs: visual, worked example, guided questions, and explanation with drills."
  - There are two timed mock midterms, a cheatsheet, a "which technique?" guide, a common-mistakes page and 70 flashcards. A progress tracker stays in the reader's own browser, with no account.

hard:
  statement: Being right. A study guide with one wrong number teaches the wrong thing.
  body: The content tests recompute every number in the worked examples, so a typo in a sum or a recurrence fails the build before anyone reads it. The notes cite slides and the textbook by number instead of copying them, and CI refuses to publish if a source PDF slips into the repository.
