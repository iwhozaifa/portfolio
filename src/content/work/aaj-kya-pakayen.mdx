problem: Every evening, the same question. The answer depends on this week's dinners, whether it's a sabzi or a gosht day, and the meat at home.
built:
  - A Flutter app in Urdu first and English second, with large text. It suggests tonight's dish with its reasons and a side, tracks chicken, beef, mutton and mince in 250 g steps, and uses the oldest meat first.
  - It remembers 60 days of dinners, holds 78 Pakistani dishes you can switch off or add to, works fully offline, and every action can be undone.
